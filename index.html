Hello! I'm Nonso, a full-stack developer with more than 4 years of hands-on experience.

I started with HTML, CSS, PHP and Laravel, building websites for clients at Orbit Brothers Communication and training at Kodex Academy. Today I build full products with React, TypeScript, Node.js and Python, and I put them online myself.

I'm a full-stack developer with a structural design background: my degree is in Architecture and Design from Nnamdi Azikiwe University. Architecture taught me to plan the structure before building, so I design systems with clear parts, and interfaces with clean layouts that are easy to use, even on a cheap phone with a weak network.
